# Return Policy — ElektroMax Sp. z o.o. (EXAMPLE — fictional document for course use)

Version: 1.0 · Applies to: consumer electronics purchased from ElektroMax retail and online stores.

## 1. Return window

- R-1: A product may be returned within **30 calendar days** from the purchase date shown on the receipt/invoice.
- R-2: Returns after 30 days are not accepted under this policy, regardless of product condition.

## 2. Product condition requirements

A returned product must satisfy ALL of the following:

- R-3: No mechanical damage (cracks, dents, deep scratches, broken elements).
- R-4: No visible signs of use: scuffs, worn keys/buttons, screen wear, dirt, residue from stickers or cases.
- R-5: Complete set: device, original accessories, cables, documentation.
- R-6: Original packaging present and undamaged (minor shelf wear acceptable).
- R-7: For devices with protective films: factory films intact or product otherwise in "as new" condition.
- R-8: The product must be resellable as new after inspection.

## 3. Exclusions — products that cannot be returned

- R-9: Hygiene-sensitive products with broken seals: in-ear headphones, personal care devices.
- R-10: Software licenses and gift cards once activated.
- R-11: Products custom-configured on customer request.
- R-12: Products with removed or damaged serial number labels.

## 4. Data and account requirements

- R-13: Devices with user accounts (phones, tablets, laptops, consoles) must be factory-reset and removed from cloud locks (e.g., account lock). A device locked to a user account is not accepted.

## 5. Refund

- R-14: Approved returns are refunded via the original payment method within 14 days of acceptance.
- R-15: If minor signs of use are found but the product is otherwise functional and complete, the employee MAY offer a partial refund (up to −20% of price) — this option requires supervisor approval (escalation).

## 6. Employee procedure (next steps after approval)

1. Verify receipt/invoice matches the product (model, date).
2. Inspect device against R-3..R-8 and R-13.
3. Register the return in the sales system and print the return confirmation.
4. Route the product to the "returns — inspection" shelf.
