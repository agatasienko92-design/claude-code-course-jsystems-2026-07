# Complaint (Warranty Claim) Policy — ElektroMax Sp. z o.o. (EXAMPLE — fictional document for course use)

Version: 1.0 · Applies to: consumer electronics purchased from ElektroMax retail and online stores.

## 1. Claim window

- C-1: Complaints are accepted within **24 months** from the purchase date shown on the receipt/invoice.
- C-2: Defects reported within the first **12 months** are presumed to have existed at the time of sale unless the damage pattern clearly indicates otherwise (see exclusions).
- C-3: After 24 months, claims are rejected under this policy.

## 2. Covered defects

A complaint is justified when the defect is attributable to the product itself:

- C-4: Manufacturing defects (faulty components, assembly errors, premature component failure).
- C-5: Functional failures not caused by the user: device does not power on, screen/display defects without impact marks, battery swelling, charging failures, dead pixels above manufacturer tolerance.
- C-6: Defects recurring after a previous repair of the same fault.

## 3. Exclusions — damage NOT covered

- C-7: Mechanical damage caused by impact or pressure: cracked screens or housings with visible impact points, dents, bent frames.
- C-8: Liquid damage: corrosion, stains, triggered liquid-contact indicators.
- C-9: Damage from misuse: use contrary to the manual, overloading, wrong power supply, unauthorized modifications.
- C-10: Normal wear: scratches, worn coatings, battery capacity degradation within manufacturer norms.
- C-11: Damage caused by unauthorized repair attempts (opened housing, missing screws, third-party parts) — voids the claim for the affected fault.
- C-12: Products with removed or damaged serial number labels.

## 4. Assessment rules

- C-13: If the visible damage pattern matches an exclusion (C-7..C-11), the claim is rejected even within the presumption period (C-2), and the rejection must name the damage pattern.
- C-14: If the photo/inspection cannot determine the cause of the defect, the case must be escalated to technical diagnosis — do not approve or reject on appearance alone.
- C-15: A claimed defect that is not visible on photos and not verifiable in conversation (e.g., "sometimes shuts down") requires technical diagnosis — escalate, do not reject.

## 5. Remedies for approved claims

Order of remedies:

- C-16: First: free repair, target 14 business days.
- C-17: If repair is impossible or fails twice: replacement with the same or equivalent model.
- C-18: If replacement is impossible: refund of the purchase price.

## 6. Employee procedure (next steps after decision)

1. Approved: register the claim in the RMA system, print the claim confirmation, pack the device for the service center.
2. Rejected: explain the specific exclusion to the customer; offer paid repair estimate if the customer wishes.
3. Escalated: attach photos and case summary; forward to the service technician queue.
